🔁 SENTENCE TRANSFORMATION · Famiglie di strutture

Riferimento Cambridge B2 First, Part 4 — 11 famiglie · la chiave dice quale struttura costruire

LE QUATTRO MOSSE

  1. Leggo la prima frase e dico che cosa significa, non che parole usa.
  2. Guardo la chiave e riconosco la famiglia: la chiave obbliga a una struttura sola.
  3. Monto la struttura attorno alla chiave, che non si tocca e non si cambia.
  4. Conto le parole (da 2 a 5) e controllo tempo, soggetto e pronomi.
In oro la parola chiave: è quella che ti viene data all'esame e che devi usare senza modificarla. Le contrazioni contano come una parola, ma se nascondono la chiave (NOT → hasn't) la risposta non vale.
1 · IL PASSIVOL1
been · being · be · by · is said to
BEENhave / has / had + been + participio passatoThey have repaired the lift. → The lift has been repaired.
BEINGam / is / are / was / were + being + participioThey are painting the hall. → The hall is being painted.
BEwill / must / can / should + be + participioThey will announce it. → It will be announced.
BYforma passiva + by + chi compie l'azioneA local architect designed it. → It was designed by a local architect.
SAID / BELIEVEDis said / is believed + to + infinitoPeople say he is rich. → He is said to be rich.
2 · FAR FARE (causative)L1
had · have · having · get + oggetto + participio
HADhad + oggetto + participio passatoA plumber repaired our boiler. → We had our boiler repaired.
HAVE / HAVINGhave / having + oggetto + participioShe is having her hair cut tomorrow.
GETget + oggetto + participio (più informale)I must get the brakes checked.
HAD (subire)had + oggetto + participio: cosa spiacevole subitaSomeone stole their laptop. → They had their laptop stolen.
3 · DISCORSO INDIRETTOL1
told · offered · denied · suggested · apologised · warned
TOLD / ADVISED / WARNED / ASKEDverbo + persona + (not) to + infinito'Don't touch it,' he said to her. → He warned her not to touch it.
OFFERED / REFUSED / PROMISEDverbo + (not) to + infinito (senza persona)'I'll help you.' → He offered to help me.
ADMITTED / DENIED / SUGGESTEDverbo + forma in -ing'I didn't do it.' → She denied doing it.
APOLOGISED FOR / ACCUSED OF / INSISTED ONverbo + preposizione + forma in -ing'I'm sorry I lied.' → He apologised for lying.
Spostamento dei tempipresente → passato, will → would, passato → past perfect'I am tired.' → She said she was tired.
4 · CONDIZIONALI E RIMPIANTIL1
unless · otherwise · as long as · wish · if only
UNLESSunless = if … not (mai due negazioni)If you don't book, you won't get a seat. → You won't get a seat unless you book.
OTHERWISEfrase, otherwise + conseguenzaTake an umbrella; otherwise you'll get soaked.
AS LONG AS / PROVIDEDcondizione postaYou can borrow it as long as you give it back.
WISH (presente)wish + past simple / couldI don't speak Spanish. → I wish I spoke Spanish.
WISH / IF ONLY (passato)wish + past perfect: rimpiantoI sold my guitar. → If only I hadn't sold my guitar.
HAD (terza condizionale)if + had + participio, would have + participioShe didn't revise, so she failed. → If she had revised, she wouldn't have failed.
5 · COMPARATIVI E SUPERLATIVIL2
as … as · than · nearly · ever · no other
AS … AS(not) as + aggettivo + asB was more boring than A. → A wasn't as boring as B.
THANmore / less / -er + thanThis café is cheaper. → This café is less expensive than the other.
NEARLY / QUITEnot nearly as … as = molto menoOtis isn't nearly as fast as Ruben.
EVERthe + superlativo + present perfect + everI've never eaten such a spicy curry. → It's the spiciest curry I've ever eaten.
NO OTHERno other + nome singolare + comparativo / superlativoNo other student works as hard as Nadia.
6 · SO · SUCH · TOO · ENOUGHL2
so … that · such a … that · too … to · not … enough
SOso + aggettivo o avverbio + thatAli was very tired, and he fell asleep. → Ali was so tired that he fell asleep.
SUCHsuch + a + aggettivo + nome + thatThe queue was so long that we left. → It was such a long queue that we left.
TOOtoo + aggettivo + (for sb) + to + infinitoThe box was too heavy for Vera to lift.
ENOUGH(not) + aggettivo + enough + to + infinitoThe box wasn't light enough for Vera to lift.
TOO MUCH / TOO FEWtoo much / many + nome · too little / few + nomeThere weren't enough chairs. → There were too few chairs.
7 · SCHEMI VERBALIL2
mind · worth · manage · prevent · stop · look forward to
MIND / AVOID / WORTH / NO POINT+ forma in -ingCould you close it? → Would you mind closing it?
MANAGED TO / SUCCEEDED INmanaged + to + infinito · succeeded in + -ingHe was able to finish. → He managed to finish.
PREVENT / STOP sb FROMprevent / stop + persona + from + -ingThe rain prevented us from playing.
STOP + -ing / STOP + tosmettere di fare / fermarsi per fareShe stopped buying it. ≠ She stopped to buy it.
LOOK FORWARD TOlook forward to + forma in -ingI'm looking forward to seeing the band.
8 · MODALI: DEDUZIONE E OBBLIGOL2
must have · can't have · might have · needn't · supposed to
MUST HAVEmust + have + participio: quasi certo di sìI'm sure he left. → He must have left.
CAN'T / COULDN'T HAVEcan't + have + participio: quasi certo di noI'm sure she didn't see it. → She can't have seen it.
MAY / MIGHT HAVEmight + have + participio: forsePerhaps she forgot. → She might have forgotten.
NEEDN'T / DON'T NEED TOnon è necessario (≠ mustn't = è vietato)It isn't necessary to bring a towel. → You needn't bring a towel.
BE SUPPOSED TO / HAD BETTERregola prevista · consiglio forteVisitors are supposed to sign. · You had better see a doctor.
9 · USED TO E ABITUDINIL3
used to · didn't use to · be used to · get used to
USED TOused to + infinito: abitudine passata finitaShe played the violin as a child. → She used to play the violin.
DIDN'T USE TO / DID … USE TOdopo did si torna a use, senza -dDid your grandmother use to work here?
BE USED TObe used to + forma in -ing: essere abituatoHe is used to living in a big city now.
GET USED TOget used to + forma in -ing: abituarsi poco a pocoI'm getting used to working nights.
USED TO BEper situazioni che non esistono piùThere used to be a cinema in our town.
10 · TEMPO, DURATA E DENAROL3
it takes · spend · for / since · ago · never … before
IT TAKESit takes + persona + tempo + to + infinitoThe walk is forty minutes long. → It takes forty minutes to walk there.
SPENDspend + tempo o denaro + on + nome / + -ingHe paid ninety euros for his boots. → He spent ninety euros on his boots.
FOR / SINCEpresent perfect + for (durata) / since (punto di partenza)She started three years ago. → She has been learning for three years.
AGO / IT'S … SINCEit's + tempo + since = tempo + agoIt's six months since it closed. → It closed six months ago.
LAST TIME / HAVEN'T … SINCEthe last time I saw him = I haven't seen him sinceI last saw Marta at the concert. → I haven't seen Marta since the concert.
NEVER … BEFOREthis is the first time = I have never … beforeThis is my first visit. → I have never visited it before.
11 · CONNETTIVI: CONTRASTO, CAUSA, SCOPOL3
despite · although · due to · in order to · avoid · meant
DESPITE / IN SPITE OF+ nome o forma in -ing (mai una frase intera)Although it rained, they played. → They played despite the rain.
ALTHOUGH / EVEN THOUGH+ soggetto + verboEven though he had a cold, he sang.
DUE TO / OWING TO / BECAUSE OF+ nome: la causaThe flight was cancelled due to the fog.
IN ORDER (NOT) TO / SO AS (NOT) TO+ infinito: lo scopoShe got up early in order to catch the ferry.
TO AVOIDto avoid + forma in -ing: scopo negativoHe spoke quietly to avoid waking the baby.
MEANT / SO … THATla conseguenzaThe road was closed, so we took a longer route. → The closed road meant we had to take a longer route.
